## Pitch day: investor pitch + BMC

**Before class**, add your group to the sign-up sheet:

- Venture name and group participants
- ID numbers without the last 3 digits
- Link to your Google Slides presentation
- Check that you're in the correct tab / hour
- Check that the presentation opens from another computer

**In class**

- 6 minutes per group (buzzer at 3 minutes, then 3 more)
- You don't need every tip from class. Add 1–2 slides that fit your company's stage or that investors will want to see.
- Language: English preferred, Hebrew is fine
