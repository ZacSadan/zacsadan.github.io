### Course outline

1. Innovation & entrepreneurship in Israel and abroad
2. The entrepreneur, the company and venture assessment
3. Creating systematic innovation (SIT)
4. Lean LaunchPad and the Business Model Canvas (BMC)
5. The investor pitch
6. Raising money
7. Patents, intellectual property and forming the company
8. Exit strategy, glossary of entrepreneurship terms, and test preparation
9. Guest lecturers — a start-up attorney, a venture-capital partner and an entrepreneur
10. Pitch day — investor pitch and BMC presented by the students
