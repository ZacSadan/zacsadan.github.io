### First meeting — and preparation for the next one

- **Business Model Canvas (BMC)**
- **Competitors slide** — direct and indirect competitors, plus a feature comparison (see the EMBA syllabus)
- **Revenue forecast** — a short projected-revenue table for the next 3 years:
    - Market size is *N* users (show supporting evidence)
    - First-year forecast, e.g. “we will reach 5% of the market: 10K users at $5 per month + 2K users at $10 per month = first-year revenue”
