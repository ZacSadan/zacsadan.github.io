**Start-up Founding (70-692-02) / Start-up Founding — Advanced (70-669-02)**
Lean LaunchPad Workshop · Zac Sadan

The workshop runs over two semesters, on Wednesdays every other week, 18:00–20:00,
in the new *Halalit* space — Bar-Ilan University's start-up hub and incubator.

[Workshop syllabus and details](https://docs.google.com/document/d/16rvNpJ8nBW-liLUDljs1RPvHJGi8-O-ILC4MjYMOOSU/?rm=minimal){: .btn }
[Preliminary registration](http://bit.ly/BIU_Inno){: .btn .btn-outline }

### Example projects from previous years

Some teams were already generating revenue by the end of the workshop:

- A nanotechnology-based blood-pressure patch (patent-backed)
- AI software for processing dental X-ray images
- A gene gun based on magnetic fields
- A social network for rating healthy products
- An AI platform for identifying passengers and vehicles
- A social venture helping older adults find employment
- Dynamic bifocal glasses
- A social venture marketing products made by people with disabilities
